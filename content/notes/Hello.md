Hello 👋

I made this portfolio as a place to show what I work on, but I also wanted somewhere to keep track of the interesting things I learn along the way.

That is what Notes is for.

I’ll use this space for anything worth writing down — projects I’m building, software I’m experimenting with, technical things I recently learned, random discoveries, and probably a few things I’ll want to remember later.

The portfolio itself was heavily inspired by two sites I really liked:

- [renato.works](https://renato.works/)
- [makos.framer.website](https://makos.framer.website/)

The final design ended up being a combination of ideas I liked from both of them, mixed with my own take on the whole desktop-style portfolio idea.

A special thanks to [Renato](https://github.com/renatoworks) as well. I used and adapted some of the code from his portfolio while building mine, and it helped a lot while putting everything together.

I’ll try to leave anything interesting I come across here, whether that is something from a project, a useful piece of software, an idea, or something I learned that might be worth sharing.

So stay tuned.
